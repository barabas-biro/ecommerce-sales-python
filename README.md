## E-Commerce Sales Analysis - Python
Ecommerce sales analysis with Python, including EDA and business insights.

## Project Overview
This project analyzes ecommerce sales data using Python. The goal is to understand revenue trends, profit performance, customer behavior, and product/category performance.

## Project Structure

The project is divided into three main parts:

1. Data Loading  
   - Loading the dataset and performing an initial inspection.

2. Data Cleaning  
   - Handling data types, missing values, duplicates, and preparing the cleaned dataset.

3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
   - Trend analysis, customer and product insights, data visualization, and business recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
The dataset contains ecommerce order data, including order details, customer information, product categories, sales, profit, quantity, discount, and payment mode.

## Analysis Performed
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Monthly profit trend analysis
- Revenue vs profit comparison
- Top customers by revenue
- Top products by revenue
- Top sub-categories by revenue
- Final business insights and recommendations

## Key Insights
- Revenue and profit show generally stable performance over time.
- The final month shows a sharp drop due to incomplete data, with only 3 days recorded.
- A small group of customers generates higher revenue, while the rest of the customer base remains relatively balanced.
- Revenue is distributed across multiple products and categories.
- Furniture, Home Decor, and Sports are among the strongest performing categories.

## Recommendation
- Focus on high-performing sub-categories such as Furniture and Sports.
- Investigate high-value, low-frequency products for premium strategy opportunities.
- Strengthen retention strategies for top revenue-generating customers.
